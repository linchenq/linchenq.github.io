I am currently a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Miami , where I am supervised by [Dr. Liang Liang](https://liangbright.wordpress.com/). My research focuses on deep learning for medical image analysis, with a particular emphasis on attention-based and VAE-based deep neural networks for geometry reconstruction from MRI and CT scans.

During my doctoral studies, my research has centered on improving the accuracy and consistency of anatomical modeling for structures such as the lumbar spine and the aortic valve. I have developed several novel architectures to address these challenges, including CQ-VAE, a generative network with a coordinate-quantized discrete latent space for shape uncertainty modeling , and attention-based shape-deformation networks like UNet-DeformSA and TransDeformer. These methods facilitate artifact-free geometry reconstruction and establish consistent inter-patient mesh correspondence, which is critical for downstream medical parameter measurement and clinical review.
